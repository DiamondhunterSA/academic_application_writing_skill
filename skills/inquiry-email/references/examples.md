# Inquiry Email Examples

These are fictional teaching examples. Names, institutions, projects, and correspondence are invented for illustration, not successful outreach cases or facts to reuse in another person's email. Use only the example relevant to the task; adapt the reasoning rather than copying the facts.

## 1. Initial Inquiry to a Prospective Supervisor

**Given material:** Mei Lin is completing a statistics degree at Birch University. In a course project, Mei cleaned public transport data and compared forecasting methods using Python; this led Mei to ask how forecasts communicate uncertainty. Professor Chen's group page describes work on uncertainty in time-series forecasts. Mei wants to ask about doctoral supervision for the 2027 intake. No paper reading, publication, funding offer, or attachment has been supplied.

The two versions below use the same material and ask about the same intake and possible fit. Both are first-contact emails. Their information emphasis differs; neither is a mandatory length or template.

### A. Background-and-Fit

**Subject:** Prospective PhD student — time-series forecasting, 2027 intake

Dear Professor Chen,

I am completing a statistics degree at Birch University and am exploring doctoral study for the 2027 intake. Your group's work on uncertainty in time-series forecasts connects with a question I would like to study further.

In a course project, I cleaned public transport data and compared forecasting methods using Python. That experience made me interested in how forecasts communicate uncertainty alongside their predictions.

Are you considering new doctoral students for that intake, and would this background be relevant to your group's work? I would be happy to provide more information about the project if useful.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Mei Lin

**Why this fits:** The connection is based on the supplied group-page summary, not a claim of reading a particular paper. The course project supports a question about possible fit without becoming a publication or independent research claim. The message asks about availability rather than assuming a position exists.

### B. Purpose-First

**Subject:** Prospective PhD student — time-series forecasting, 2027 intake

Dear Professor Chen,

Are you considering new doctoral students for the 2027 intake, and might my background be relevant to your group's work on time-series uncertainty? I am completing a statistics degree at Birch University and am interested in doctoral study in this area.

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Mei Lin

**Why this fits:** The main request is visible immediately, with the degree, institution, research area, and intake needed to interpret it. The project account is omitted without changing the applicant's background or the core question. This approach can suit an initial inquiry; it does not imply that the sender lacks relevant experience.

**Using the pair:** Choose according to the user's request and relevant preferences, following [formats.md](formats.md#1-initial-contact-with-a-prospective-supervisor). Provide both when a comparison is useful under that guidance; do not automatically produce two drafts for every inquiry. Neither version adds a claim of reading a paper or attaching a file.

## 2. Eligibility Question to an Admissions Office

**Given material:** Arun Patel is completing an engineering degree and is considering the MSc in Applied Statistics at Harbor University. The supplied requirements mention prior probability study but do not identify which courses qualify. Arun completed Engineering Probability; its syllabus is available, but the user has not asked to attach it. Arun needs clarification before deciding whether to apply.

**Subject:** MSc Applied Statistics — probability prerequisite clarification

Dear Admissions Team,

I am considering applying to the MSc in Applied Statistics and would like to clarify the probability prerequisite. I am completing an engineering degree and have taken a course titled Engineering Probability.

Could you advise whether that course could satisfy the prerequisite, or how I should request a course-content review? I can provide its syllabus if needed.

Thank you for your guidance.

Best regards,
Arun Patel

**Why this fits:** An administrative question goes to the admissions office with the exact requirement and relevant course. The message requests clarification without claiming eligibility or demanding an admissions decision. It offers a syllabus without saying one is attached.

## 3. Follow-Up in an Existing Thread

**Given material:** Sofia Reyes previously asked Professor Ali about a visiting research opportunity and provided the intended visit dates. The user now requests a follow-up in the same thread. The recipient has not responded, no stated response period or no-contact instruction applies, and no new deadline or achievement has been supplied. The interval has been judged appropriate from the actual thread; this example does not establish a universal waiting period.

**Subject:** Keep the existing subject

Dear Professor Ali,

I am following up on my inquiry below about a possible visiting research opportunity. Could you let me know whether you are considering visitors for the period I mentioned, or whether another contact would be more appropriate?

Thank you for your time.

Best regards,
Sofia Reyes

**Why this fits:** The earlier message supplies the background and dates. The follow-up briefly restores the question without inventing urgency, claiming the email was overlooked, or repeating the full pitch. A refusal or request not to follow up would change whether another inquiry is appropriate.

## Wording Changes Without Adding Facts

Given only a confirmed course project in time-series forecasting and a group-page summary on forecast uncertainty:

- **Inflated:** "I have followed your groundbreaking papers for years, and I am the perfect candidate for your world-renowned laboratory."
- **Grounded:** "Your group's work on forecast uncertainty connects with questions I encountered while comparing forecasting methods in a course project."

The revision removes unsupported history and evaluation. If no actual connection has been supplied, ask for it or use a factual inquiry without a personalization claim; do not invent a connection to complete the sentence.
